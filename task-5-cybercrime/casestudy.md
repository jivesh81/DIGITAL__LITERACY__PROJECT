# Case Study: UPI / Online Payment Fraud
## Task 5 – Cybercrime Awareness | CSE0001 Digital Literacy

---

## What is UPI Fraud?

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) fraud is one of the fastest-growing cybercrime categories
in India. It is a form of financial fraud where attackers manipulate victims into
voluntarily transferring money or sharing sensitive payment credentials — such as UPI PINs
or OTPs — through social engineering rather than technical hacking.

---

## How It Happens — Step by Step

**A Realistic Scenario:**

Rahul, a first-year engineering student in Bhopal, listed his old laptop for sale on
OLX for Rs. 18,000. Within an hour, he received a message from someone claiming to be
an Army officer interested in buying it urgently.

1. The "buyer" said he would pay via UPI immediately and asked for Rahul's UPI ID.

2. He then sent Rahul a QR code saying — *"Scan this to receive your payment of Rs. 18,000."*

3. Rahul, unfamiliar with how QR codes work in UPI, scanned the code and entered his
   UPI PIN when prompted — believing he was receiving money.

4. In reality, scanning the QR code and entering the PIN authorised a payment **out** of
   Rahul's account. Rs. 18,000 was instantly debited.

5. The fraudster immediately blocked Rahul on all platforms and became unreachable.

---

## Who Is Usually Targeted?

- First-year college students new to UPI apps
- People selling items on OLX, Facebook Marketplace, or Quikr
- Senior citizens unfamiliar with digital payment flows
- Job seekers asked to "register" by paying a small refundable fee

---

## Consequences

- Immediate financial loss — often with no possibility of reversal
- Psychological distress, shame, and loss of trust in digital platforms
- No guarantee of legal recovery since the transfer was technically authorised by the victim
- In some cases, victims are targeted again after being identified as susceptible
