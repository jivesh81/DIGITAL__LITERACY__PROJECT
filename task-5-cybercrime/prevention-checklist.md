# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist
## Task 5 – Cybercrime Awareness | CSE0001 Digital Literacy

---

### General Online Safety

1. **Never share your OTP with anyone**
   No bank, UPI app, telecom company, or government body will ever call or message you
   asking for an OTP. If someone asks — it is a scam, no exceptions.

2. **Use strong, unique passwords for every account**
   A strong password is at least 10 characters long and includes uppercase letters,
   lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Never reuse the same password across platforms.

3. **Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on all accounts**
   Turn on 2FA for your email, GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, and any platform that supports
   it. This adds a second layer of security even if your password is stolen.

4. **Always verify the URL before logging in**
   Phishing websites look identical to real ones but have slightly different URLs
   (e.g., sbi-login.net instead of sbi.co.in). Always check the address bar before
   entering any credentials.

5. **Do not click on unknown links in SMS, WhatsApp, or email**
   Fraudsters send fake links disguised as bank alerts, prize notifications, or KYC
   renewal notices. When in doubt, go directly to the official website instead of clicking.

6. **Keep your apps and operating system updated**
   Security patches fix known vulnerabilities. Always install updates for your phone,
   laptop, and apps — especially banking and UPI apps.

---

### UPI and Financial Safety (Specific to College Students in India)

7. **Scanning a QR code sends money — it does not receive it**
   This is the most common misconception exploited by fraudsters. You should NEVER enter
   your UPI PIN when someone claims to be sending you money. A PIN is only required when
   you are making a payment.

8. **Never accept payment "requests" from strangers on UPI**
   UPI apps allow sending payment requests. If you receive a request from an unknown
   number — even if the amount matches what you are owed — decline it and verify with the
   sender directly over a phone call before approving.

### How to Report Cybercrime in India

| Channel | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **National Cyber Crime Portal** | [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in) — file a complaint online |
| **Cyber Crime Helpline** | **1930** — call 24x7, available across India |
| **Local Police** | Visit your nearest police station and file an FIR |
| **RBI Ombudsman** | For banking and UPI-related financial fraud |

> Report immediately — the sooner you report, the higher the chance of recovery.
