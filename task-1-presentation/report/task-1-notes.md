For Task 1, I created a Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic using Canva. The infographic was designed as a one-page visual resource to help students understand the meaning and importance of digital literacy. I included key topics such as safe internet practices, useful digital tools for students, and professional online presence. The visual layout was kept clean and attractive with icons, headings, and short points so that it could be quickly understood by my batchmates.

The most interesting part of this task was converting theoretical ideas into a visually engaging design. It helped me realize that digital awareness content becomes much more effective when presented in a simple and creative format. This task improved my ability to communicate educational content visually and strengthened my understanding of how design tools can be used for academic awareness campaigns.
