# Social Media Do's and Don'ts
## Responsible Social Media Use for College Students

---

### DO's

1. **Keep your profile professional** — Use your real name and a decent photo on LinkedIn
   and GitHub. Employers and recruiters check these platforms regularly.

2. **Think before you post** — Ask yourself: would you be comfortable if your professor,
   parent, or future employer saw this? Your digital footprint is permanent.

3. **Use social media for learning** — Follow researchers, professors, and companies in
   your field. Platforms like LinkedIn and Twitter are excellent for staying updated on
   industry trends.

4. **Engage respectfully in online discussions** — Express disagreement politely and
   constructively. How you communicate online reflects your character.

5. **Report abusive or spam content** — Use the platform's report feature to flag
   harassment, misinformation, or spam. This helps keep the community safe.

---

### DON'Ts

1. **Never share personal details publicly** — Avoid posting your phone number, home
   address, Aadhaar number, or UPI ID on any public social media platform.

2. **Do not share unverified news or rumours** — Always verify information from a
   credible source before forwarding or reposting. Spreading misinformation can have
   serious consequences.

3. **Do not post offensive, discriminatory, or inappropriate content** — Even if deleted
   later, screenshots last forever. Offensive posts can damage academic and career prospects.

4. **Do not use social media during lectures or exams** — It is disrespectful to peers
   and professors and directly affects your academic performance.

5. **Never share your login credentials or OTP with anyone** — Not with friends, not
   with strangers online, and not with anyone claiming to be from your college or a bank.
   Legitimate organisations never ask for passwords or OTPs.
